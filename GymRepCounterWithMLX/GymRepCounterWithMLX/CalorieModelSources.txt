CALORIE MODEL — SOURCES & RATIONALE
===================================

This documents the research behind the per-rep calorie estimate used in the
Workout Report (Live from Gallery). The model lives in ReportView.swift
(WorkoutReport calorie section + UserBodyProfile).


EQUATION
--------
Per exercise:

    kcal(exercise) = reps × MET(exercise) × RMR(kcal/min) × repDuration(min)

Personalised resting metabolic rate (Mifflin-St Jeor BMR), which brings
weight, height and sex into the estimate:

    RMR(kcal/min) = BMR_per_day / 1440
    BMR_per_day   = 10·W(kg) + 6.25·H(cm) − 5·age + sexConstant
                    sexConstant = +5 (male), −161 (female)
                    age = 30 (fixed adult default; not collected from the user)

Rationale: the standard ACSM/Compendium formula
(kcal/min = MET × 3.5 × weight / 200) depends on weight alone. Replacing its
fixed 3.5 ml/kg/min resting term with a personalised Mifflin-St Jeor RMR makes
the result also depend on height and sex, as required.


MET VALUES (2024 Compendium of Physical Activities)
---------------------------------------------------
    pushup      7.5   calisthenics, vigorous effort
    pullup      8.0   calisthenics, vigorous (upper-body, demanding)
    squat       5.0   resistance training, squats
    skipping   11.0   rope skipping, general
    legraise    6.5   body-weight resistance, high intensity (core)
    squatpress  6.0   body-weight circuit / compound movement


PER-REP TEMPO (approximate seconds per rep)
-------------------------------------------
    pushup 2.0 | pullup 3.0 | squat 3.0 | skipping 0.5 | legraise 3.0 | squatpress 3.5


DEFAULTS (used when an input is left blank)
-------------------------------------------
    weight = 70 kg    (classic "reference man")
    height = 170 cm   (≈ global adult average)
    gender = male     (Mifflin-St Jeor +5 baseline)


SOURCES
-------
2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities (MET values):
    https://pacompendium.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/1_2024-adult-compendium_1_2024.pdf
    https://pacompendium.com/conditioning-exercise/

ACSM MET calorie formula (MET × 3.5 × weight / 200; ~5 kcal per litre O2):
    https://www.calculator.net/calories-burned-calculator.html

Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation (weight, height, age, sex):
    https://www.omnicalculator.com/health/bmr

Average adult height / weight (NCD Risk Factor Collaboration / Our World in Data):
    https://ourworldindata.org/human-height


NOTES / APPROXIMATIONS
----------------------
- Age is not collected; fixed at 30.
- Gender is a 2-option control (male/female); the default (male) applies when
  the user does not change it.
- MET values are population averages; per-rep tempos are assumptions. Results
  are estimates, not measurements.
